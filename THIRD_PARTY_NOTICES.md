## Japanese three-garden layout reference

The Japanese demonstration, **三庭 · 静居 / NIWA TO MA**, is a manually authored parametric design adaptation informed by the published plan for a single-storey residence in Neyagawa, Osaka (listing building area 221.51 m²; land 477.56 m²). The source is a [MASH property flyer](https://mash-c.jp/wp/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/%E5%AF%9D%E5%B1%8B%E5%B7%9D%E5%B8%82%E5%85%AB%E5%B9%A1%E5%8F%B06-17%E3%80%8012800%E4%B8%87%E5%86%86%EF%BC%89.pdf). The original flyer, drawing, photographs, names and marks remain their owners' property and are outside this project's MIT license. They are not redistributed. No affiliation or endorsement is implied.

The generated scene follows studied room adjacencies, three gardens and an engawa corridor. Room dimensions, heights, roof forms, fittings, furnishings and planting are project-authored estimates. Its stated area is a design target derived from the listing, not a new measurement. Published Japanese sample images are browser captures of the generated 3D model, not property photographs or native path-traced renders.
